# Gemini Canvas Prompt: Clash Royale Shareable Player Card

## Prompt

Create a sleek, modern shareable player card design inspired by Spotify Wrapped, but themed around Clash Royale and university campus competition. The card should be 1200x1200 pixels (square format) with a dark, premium aesthetic.

**Design Elements:**

**Background:**
- Dark gradient background (deep royal blue #003DA5 transitioning to blue-tinted dark #0F1B2E)
- Subtle grid pattern or arena-like texture overlay
- Clash Royale character silhouettes or illustrations in the background (faded, low opacity) - include iconic characters like the King, Queen, or popular troops like the Knight, Archer, or Wizard
- Gold accent elements (#FFD700) for premium feel

**Layout Structure:**

**Top Section:**
- Large crown icon or Clash Royale king/queen character illustration (prominent, stylized)
- Player name: "KingSlayer_99" in bold, white text
- University name: "Stanford University" in smaller gray text below
- Player tag: "#2YPQVV8P" in monospace font, subtle

**Center Section (Hero Stats):**
- Large, prominent display of "CAMPUS #1" in gold text with decorative border/box
- Ranking Score: "87.5" displayed prominently with "Ranking Score" label
- Path of Legends League: "Ultimate Champion" in stylized text with league badge/icon

**Stats Grid:**
- Trophy count: "7,842" with trophy icon
- Win Rate: "68%" with percentage indicator
- Three Crown Rate: "12%" 
- Best Trophies: "8,200"
- Display these in a clean grid or card layout with icons

**Bottom Section:**
- Current deck preview: Show 8 Clash Royale card icons in a row (small, recognizable card art)
- Favorite card: Highlight one card with "Favorite Card" label
- Small "clashcampus.com" branding at the very bottom

**Visual Style:**
- Modern, clean typography with bold sans-serif fonts
- Clash Royale color palette: royal blue (#003DA5), secondary blue (#2D85F3), gold (#FFD700), dark backgrounds
- Glowing effects on key stats (ranking score, campus rank)
- Card should feel premium and shareable - like a digital achievement badge
- Include subtle Clash Royale character art (King, Queen, or troops) integrated into the design - perhaps as decorative elements, borders, or background characters
- Use Clash Royale's signature art style: bold, colorful, game-like aesthetic

**Character Integration:**
- Feature the Clash Royale King or Queen character prominently (either as a large illustration on one side, or as a decorative element)
- Include smaller character illustrations of popular troops (Knight, Archer, Wizard, etc.) as background elements or decorative borders
- Characters should enhance the "king/queen of campus" theme
- Make it feel like the player is being crowned or celebrated by these characters

**Overall Feel:**
- Should look like a premium, shareable social media card
- "Rise of Kingdoms" power level aesthetic - showing dominance and achievement
- Spotify Wrapped energy but with Clash Royale personality
- Make it feel like a victory celebration or coronation moment

**Technical Notes:**
- 1200x1200 pixels, square format
- High contrast for social media sharing
- Readable text at small sizes (for Instagram/Twitter thumbnails)
- Include visual hierarchy: most important stats are largest

## Alternative Shorter Prompt

Create a 1200x1200 pixel shareable player card for "Clash Campus" - a university Clash Royale ranking system. Design a dark, premium card (royal blue #003DA5 and gold #FFD700 color scheme) featuring:

- Large Clash Royale King or Queen character illustration (prominent, stylized)
- Player name "KingSlayer_99" and "Stanford University"
- Hero stat: "CAMPUS #1" in gold with ranking score "87.5"
- Stats: 7,842 Trophies, 68% Win Rate, Ultimate Champion league
- Current deck: 8 Clash Royale card icons
- Background: Dark gradient with subtle Clash Royale character silhouettes (Knight, Archer, Wizard)
- Style: Spotify Wrapped meets Clash Royale - modern, shareable, premium achievement card
- Theme: "King/Queen of Campus" - coronation/victory celebration vibe

Make it feel like a digital trophy card that players would want to share on Instagram or Twitter.

## Variations to Try

### Variation 1: Power Level Theme
Add a "Power Level" meter or gauge showing the player's dominance, similar to Rise of Kingdoms ads. Include level progression visuals.

### Variation 2: Battle Card Style
Make it look like an in-game Clash Royale card with borders, rarity indicators, and card-like framing.

### Variation 3: Seasonal Recap
Add "This Season" highlights, battle count, rank changes, with a more narrative/storytelling layout.

### Variation 4: Minimalist
Clean, minimal design with just essential stats and one prominent character (King or Queen).
